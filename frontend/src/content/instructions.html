The movie quiz game with over half a million questions!

All questions are in the form "Who in XXX starred in another movie with YYY?" and you get one point for each correct answer. A single incorrect answer will end the game, but to your help you have a couple of lifelines, RAG tokens and GraphRAG tokens.

RAG tokens can be used to get a clue about the movie asked about (XXX above). Spending a RAG token will enable the clue card for the movie and if you click it you will see up to 5 actors credited in that movie. The answer will be one of those. You have 2 RAG tokens to your disposal.

GraphRAG tokens will traverse all the way from the movie asked about (XXX) to the actor (YYY) and will show you a clue card for the movie that both YYY and the asked about actor starred in. You can flip both this clue card as well as the clue card for the movie asked about (XXX) and cross-check the list of actors in both of them. You have 2 GraphRAG tokens to your disposal.

You can zoom in on the clue cards by right-clicking them, or by focusing them and pressing Z.

Before starting a game you have to enter your name (or nickname). This is only used for the highscore list (showing the top 3 highscores), and will be deleted when the game is over unless you made the highscore list. A game that is started but not finished or closed will remain in the backend for 3 days after the last action and will then be deleted.

Don't cheat by having IMDB in another tab!! ;)

What is GraphRAG?

RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) is a method to combine the power of large language models with external knowledge sources. When a question is asked to an LLM the system will first go to the knowledge source to retrieve what seems to be relevant information, usually using vector search, and provide that to the LLM as context for generating a more informed answer.
With GraphRAG that knowledge source is a graph database, which allows for a traversal of the graph from the documents found from the initial vector search to find more relevant documents and provide a much richer context to the LLM.
The purpose of this game, except to be a fun quiz, is to demonstrate what RAG and GraphRAG is and why GraphRAG often creates a stronger context.

Where do the questions come from?

As stated above there are over half a million questions in the game, but they are not predefined. Instead the game is backed by a Neo4j graph database containing all famous (see below) movies and actors from the public imdb dataset, with a schema like this:

(:Person)-[:ACTED_IN|:DIRECTED|:PRODUCED|:COMPOSED|:WRITTEN]->(:Movie)

Every time a question is asked the system will find a random pattern in the following form:

MATCH (p1:Person)-[a1:ACTED_IN]->(m1:Movie)<-[a2:ACTED_IN]-(p2:Person)-[a3:ACTED_IN]->(m2:Movie)

However, we must also make sure that there is only one answer to the question, so we will run the query with this predicate:

WHERE p1<>p2 AND m1<>m2 AND a1.order < 6 AND a2.order < 6 AND a3.order < 6
AND NOT EXISTS {
     (p1)-[:ACTED_IN]->(:Movie)<-[:ACTED_IN]-(p3:Person)-[:ACTED_IN]->(m2)
     WHERE p3 <> p1 AND p3 <> p2
}

So in a sense we use the power of graphs to demonstrate the power of graphs...

What is a famous movie and actor?

There are about 13 million movies, series, episodes, specials, video games in the public imdb dataset, and about 18 million persons. Including all of these would make the game extremely hard (it is, as you will see, hard enough as it is). Therefore we have included only movies, and only if they have been determined to be famous, which means:

  • It has at least 1000 votes on IMDB
  • It has an English Wikipedia page, as well as at least 25 pages in other languages
  • It is not an adult movie

    • An actor is considered famous if:

  • It has a "Known For" connecting it to at least one famous movie
  • It has an English Wikipedia page, as well as at least 25 pages in other languages.

    • What this means is that there may be a different answer to a question. It could be that someone else from XXX participated with YYY, but in a movie that wasn't deemed famous, and thus will not be included in the game.

    An important thing to note is that the movie data was pulled on June 15th 2026, so only movies before that are included in the game.
